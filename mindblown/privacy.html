Mind Blown privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

I'm the developer of Mind Blown. This policy covers the Android game.

Your progress is saved on your phone and isn't sent to me. The game uses Google AdMob for optional ads, which does involve some data collection (explained below).

Your progress

Your scores, Campaign progress, diamonds, cosmetics and settings stay on your phone. I don't run a server for your saves.

Android may back up your saves to your Google account and restore them on a new phone. This is controlled in your phone's backup settings.

Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes the saves on your phone. Backups are managed separately through Android's backup settings.

Ads

You can choose to watch an ad for various things, such as for diamonds or to continue a run. There are no banner or pop-up ads.

Google AdMob collects and shares data to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. This can include device identifiers (such as your advertising ID), your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), app and ad interactions, and diagnostics such as app performance. AdMob encrypts this data in transit.

At startup, the game checks your ad choices with Google and starts the ad software. It requests an ad only when you open the shop or are offered a continue. Choosing not to watch ads doesn't necessarily prevent all data collection by Google's software.

In the UK and the European Economic Area, Google's consent message asks for your choices before your first ad, and you can change them at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On any phone, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

See how Google uses data from apps, Google's privacy policy and its retention and deletion policy for details.

Purchases

Google Play handles diamond purchases. Google confirms each purchase to the game so it can add your diamonds.

Children

Mind Blown isn't made for children under 13.

Emailing me

If you email me, naturally I'll use your email address and message to reply and help with your query. These emails are handled by Gmail.

I manage this inbox myself and don't have a fixed schedule for deleting emails. You can ask me to delete your emails by contacting me (see below).

Changes

I'll update this page and the date above if/when this policy changes.
